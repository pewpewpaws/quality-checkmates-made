# Feature Brief: Smart Charging Scheduler
Author: Elena Rostova
Target Release: v3.4

Acceptance Criteria:
- The mobile app shall display real-time state of charge when connected via Bluetooth Low Energy.
- The charging controller should automatically throttle charging current if pack temperature exceeds 45 degrees Celsius.
- The system must resume nominal charge rate within 60 seconds of temperature returning to safe limits.
- The backend shall calculate estimated time to full charge and push notifications to the owner.
- The vehicle should minimize grid load spikes during peak tariff hours where appropriate.
