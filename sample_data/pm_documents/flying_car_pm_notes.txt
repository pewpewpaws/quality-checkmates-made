# Project SkyGlide: Urban Flying Car PRD & Feature Notes
Author: Jordan Rivera (Principal Product Manager, Mobility)
Status: Early Draft for Engineering Review

## Flight Experience & Passenger Comfort
- The aircraft should take off vertically from city vertiports without producing excessive rotor noise.
- When transitioning from vertical hover to forward flight, the flight controller needs to make the tilt transition as smooth and quick as possible.
- The autopilot must never bank too steeply during turns so passenger drinks do not spill.
- The flight control system shall maintain passenger ride comfort and minimize turbulence during strong wind gusts.
- The cabin climate control system should cool or warm the interior to a pleasant temperature before passenger boarding.

## Energy, Battery & Charging
- The high-voltage battery system must provide sufficient flight range for typical metropolitan commutes with ample reserve for emergency diversions.
- The rapid charger needs to recharge the battery pack fast enough between passenger drop-offs without overheating the cells.
- If the battery state-of-charge drops to a critical level, the aircraft shall immediately reroute to the nearest safe landing site.
- The vehicle should charge efficiently while connected to commercial urban vertiport power grids.

## Autonomous Safety & Collision Avoidance
- The sensor suite must detect other aircraft, construction cranes, and drones from a safe distance and execute an evasive maneuver immediately.
- In case of catastrophic motor failure, the safety system shall deploy a ballistic parachute fast enough to prevent high-impact ground collision.
- The vehicle doors must stay locked whenever the aircraft is airborne or while rotor blades are spinning.
- The ground radar should detect obstacles on the landing pad and stop the taxi wheels within a reasonable distance.
- The aircraft shall land gently on vertiport landing pads even during sudden crosswinds.
