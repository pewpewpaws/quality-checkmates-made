# Product Requirement Document (PRD): Next-Gen EV Battery Management System (BMS)
Author: Sarah Jenkins (Lead Product Manager, Powertrain)
Target Release: Q3 2027
Status: Draft Review

1. Overview
The BMS oversees high-voltage pack safety, thermal balancing, and vehicle telemetry.

2. Functional Requirements
1. The BMS shall monitor all individual cell voltages every 50ms and report anomalies immediately.
2. The thermal management system should quickly cool the pack when temperature exceeds normal limits.
3. The battery contactors shall disconnect high voltage within [TBD: disconnect_time_ms] upon crash detection.
4. The system shall periodically upload battery state-of-health telemetry to the cloud where feasible.
5. The BMS shall calculate remaining state-of-charge with high accuracy and update the digital instrument cluster.
6. The BMS shall monitor all individual cell voltages every 50ms and report anomalies immediately.
7. The accumulator management subsystem shall measure each series cell potential at 50ms periodic intervals and report out-of-range readings to the main ECU.
8. Upon impact or crash detection, the battery contactors shall remain energized and closed to supply continuous auxiliary steering and hazard lighting.
9. The system shall maintain complete offline isolation and transmit zero cloud telemetry under any circumstances during vehicle operation.
10. The cabin ambient footwell LED lighting shall illuminate in amber whenever any passenger door is opened.
